## 4. Relationship Between Sites

Both locations belong to the Western Jezero Delta
Front and Hawksbill Gap region.

NASA reports the outcrops are approximately
20 metres apart.

The sites provide two complementary sedimentary
targets for comparative geological investigation.

## 5. Proposed Human Science Mission

Objective:
Compare adjacent sedimentary facies to investigate
ancient water deposition, sediment provenance,
mineralogy, and water-rock alteration.

Proposed EVA activities:
1. Survey sedimentary layering.
2. Compare sandstone and mudstone.
3. Inspect mineral and alteration features.
4. Record rock and terrain observations.
5. Map safe access and return routes.

This is a proposed human EVA concept, not an
official NASA astronaut mission.
